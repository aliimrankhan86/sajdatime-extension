SajdaTime privacy policy

For the SajdaTime browser extension for Chrome and Edge. Last updated 27 October 2026.

SajdaTime shows prayer times in your browser. It is made to work without an account, without ads and without sending your information to a server.

The short version. Your place and settings are saved on this computer. The extension makes no network requests. It has no analytics and no advertising. When you choose Use my location, the browser supplies the coordinates and SajdaTime keeps them in local storage. SajdaTime does not send them anywhere. The city list is built into the extension, so finding a town is also offline.

What SajdaTime stores

The extension uses the browser's local extension storage, chrome.storage.local, for:

  • the place you chose, including its name, latitude, longitude, time zone and country code,
  • your calculation method, Asr school and Hijri date shift,
  • your alert choice and other display settings when those screens are added.

This information stays in the browser profile on the computer where you installed the extension. It is not synced to your Google account by SajdaTime. Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage, subject to the browser's own behaviour.

Location

Location is optional. If you choose Use my location, the browser asks for permission and passes SajdaTime a latitude and longitude. SajdaTime stores those coordinates locally, rounded to about one kilometre, so it can work out prayer times for your place. If you refuse, or the browser cannot find a place, the extension offers Use Makkah for now and says clearly that the times are for Makkah.

Prayer times are calculated on the computer. SajdaTime does not send the coordinates to any server of its own or of anyone else.

The browser's own location service is separate from SajdaTime. Depending on your browser, operating system and settings, it may use wifi, mobile networks or a network location service to estimate where you are. SajdaTime does not control that service and cannot promise that the coordinates the browser gives it were worked out without a network request.

What SajdaTime does not do

  • No accounts, sign in or profile.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage counts.
  • No advertising, tracking pixels or advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No remote code. The files that run are the files installed with the extension.
  • No reading of the pages you visit. The extension has no content scripts and no host permissions.

Permissions

The extension asks only for what the visible features need:

  • storage for the place and settings described above,
  • geolocation so Use my location can work when you choose it.

Prayer alerts are not in this build, so the extension does not ask for alarm, notification or audio permission yet. Those permissions will be added only with a working alert, and this page will be updated first.

SajdaTime does not ask for access to your tabs, your browsing history, your bookmarks or the content of any web page.

Third parties

The extension bundles the adhan-js prayer time library. It runs locally. SajdaTime uses no third party analytics, advertising or cloud service.

The offline city search uses data from GeoNames under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. That data is shipped inside the extension and is not contacted at run time.

If you install SajdaTime from the Chrome Web Store or the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store, the store operator processes the install and may collect information under its own privacy policy. That is outside SajdaTime's control.

Children

SajdaTime is a general audience utility. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date at the top. A change that affects what is stored or sent will also be described in the extension's release notes.

Who is responsible

SajdaTime is made by Ali Imran Khan. Questions about privacy can be raised at github.com/aliimrankhan86/sajdatime-extension/issues. That page is public, so please do not put private information there.

SajdaTime is a separate product from the SajdaTime Android app and has its own policy.
